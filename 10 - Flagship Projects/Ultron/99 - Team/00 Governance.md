# Team Governance Model

1. **Architecture Authority**: Held exclusively by `Ultron.ArchitectureLead`.
2. **AI Reasoning Domain**: Led by `Ultron.AIEngineer`.
3. **Documentation Authority**: Led by `Documentation AI` / Lead Compiler.
4. **Contributors**: Must sign and adhere to `Development Agreement.md` and pass zero-drift PR review checks.
